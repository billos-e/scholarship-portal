===========================
The system should be easy for non-technical staff to manage and designed so we can expand it in the future.

Student Portal
Students should have secure login access and only be able to view and edit their own information.
The portal should allow students to:

View and update their profile
Submit semester tuition payment requests
Upload tuition invoices (PDF or image)
Upload academic transcripts
Complete a semester progress report
View the status of their payment request (Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Paid)
View previous semester submissions and payment history
