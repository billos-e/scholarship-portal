# **Proposal: Student Scholarship Portal and Management System** 

## **Prepared for: Alexandra Bay** 

**Prepared by: TECHMA** 

**Project Cost: $1,500 USD** 

## **Platform: Full custom build in Replit** 

## **Project Overview** 

Based on the requirements provided and our recent call, TECHMA will build a secure, easy-to-manage student portal and admin dashboard to help your organization manage student scholarship information, tuition payment requests, semester reports, academic records, document uploads, and payment tracking in one centralized system. 

The goal of this project is to replace the current manual spreadsheet and email-based process with a simple, structured platform that can be managed by non-technical staff and expanded in the future as your organization’s needs grow. 

The full system will be built in Replit, including the student portal, admin dashboard, database structure, authentication, file upload handling, and deployment. 

## **Project Goals** 

The system will allow your organization to: 

- Manage all student records in one centralized platform 

- Give students secure login access to submit and update their own information 

- Collect tuition payment requests each semester 

- Collect invoices, transcripts, semester reports, and wellbeing information 

- Allow administrators to review, approve, and track payments 

- Reduce manual data entry and spreadsheet management 

- Export data when needed for reporting or internal review 

- Maintain a scalable structure that can support future features

## **Scope of Work** 

## **1. Student Portal** 

TECHMA will build a secure student-facing portal where each student can log in and access only their own information. 

Students will be able to: 

- View and update their profile 

- Submit semester tuition payment requests 

- Upload tuition invoices in PDF or image format 

- Upload academic transcripts 

- Complete a semester progress report 

- View the status of their payment request 

- View previous semester submissions and payment history 

Payment request statuses will include: 

- Submitted 

- Under Review • Approved 

- Paid 

## **2. Student Profile Management** 

Each student profile will include: 

- Name • Student ID • Email 

- Phone Number 

- University 

- Degree Program 

- Year of Study 

- Current Semester 

- GPA 

- Student status, such as Active, Graduated, or Inactive 

The structure will allow your organization to disable student access when a student graduates or is no longer part of the program, while still keeping historical data for internal records. 

## **3. Tuition Payment Request Form** 

Each semester, students will be able to submit a tuition payment request with the required financial and academic information. 

2 

The tuition payment request will include: 

- Amount Due 

- Due Date 

- Tuition invoice upload 

- Bank Account Name 

- Bank Account Number 

- Bank Name 

- PromptPay Number, optional 

- QR payment image upload, if needed 

- Semester Report 

## **4. Semester Report** 

Students will complete a short semester progress report as part of their submission. 

## **Academic Information** 

- GPA 

- Credits completed • Confirmation of whether the student passed all courses • Transcript upload 

## **Wellbeing Survey** 

Students will answer simple rating questions from 1 to 5 for: 

- Physical wellbeing • Mental wellbeing • Financial wellbeing • Stress level • Confidence in completing studies 

## **Challenges** 

Students will be able to select any challenges they experienced, including: 

- Financial difficulties • Family responsibilities 

- Mental health 

- Housing 

- Transportation 

- Technology 

- Health 

- Other 

3 

## **Activities** 

Students will be able to select activities they participated in, including: 

- Community service 

- Volunteering 

- Leadership 

- Internship 

- Part-time work 

- Student clubs 

## **Reflection Questions** 

Students will answer 2 to 3 short written questions: 

- Greatest achievement this semester 

- Biggest challenge and how they overcame it 

- Anything they would like the organization to know 

## **5. Admin Dashboard** 

TECHMA will build an admin dashboard for your team to manage students and review submissions. 

Administrators will be able to: 

- View all students 

- Search by student name 

- Search or filter by university 

- Filter by semester 

- Filter by payment status 

- View uploaded transcripts 

- View uploaded tuition invoices 

- View bank details 

- Review semester reports 

- Approve payment requests 

- Mark payments as Paid 

- Add internal notes 

- Export data to Excel or CSV 

The admin dashboard will be designed to be simple and easy for non-technical staff to use. 

## **6. Database Structure Built in Replit** 

Instead of using Airtable, the system will include a custom database structure built as part of the Replit application. 

The database will be designed to be clean, scalable, and easy to expand in the future.
